# v0.1 release notes (draft)

Status: draft, not final. Built from `release` head `d225632` plus any later fix; final version records the commit the zip came from. Free, browser build first.

## What you get

- One level to fly from takeoff to landing: roll along the runway, lift off with speed and nose-up, pass the checkpoint rings, fly through the finish gate and land.
- A 1920s biplane. Throttle sets engine power and stays where you leave it. With power off the plane glides down; too slow and it stalls and falls.
- Turns come from banking: bank and pull up to turn, steeper at speed. A banked plane loses height unless you pitch up. On the ground the plane only steers while rolling.
- Obstacles. Red balls crash the plane; green treetops and bushes damage it. Trees and rocks are only scenery. A damaged plane shows a bent, scorched aileron and rudder (Medium and High).
- Hard landings and crashes break the plane apart and show a crash screen with Restart and Menu.
- A results screen with time, checkpoints, clean or damaged, a medal and 1-3 stars, plus your best time for the session.
- Coloured terrain with hills, trees and rocks, clouds, speed streaks, rain and snow. Low, Medium and High quality presets (Low by default in the browser; F1 cycles on desktop).
- Sounds: engine that follows power, wind that rises with speed, checkpoint chime, touchdown thump, crash, menu click. Master volume.
- Plays on desktop and in a phone or desktop browser. Touch: left-side stick, throttle buttons, restart button. A fullscreen button and a landscape hint are on the web page.

## Known issues

- Flight feel has not been tuned by a person yet. High-speed turns are wide (about 262 m radius at 50 m/s with a 45 degree bank); full roll without pulling up sinks fast; slow-speed turns are sharp.
- Landing from top speed needs an early power cut: cutting at the gate rolls out about 500 m, past the landing zone.
- Phones: touch controls, audio start and frame rate have not been checked on a real phone. iPhone Safari is untested.
- Best time and quality preset are not saved in the browser; best time is kept only until you reload.
- Damage look: the bend direction and scorch strength may look off; pieces that fly off in a crash do not show the damage.
- Snow flakes are hard-edged squares and can look chunky; smoke puffs are blocky when close.
- Trees and rocks have no collision: you can fly through them.
- There is no way to change the quality preset on touch devices.
- Sound has not been reviewed by ear on every device.
- iOS app: not available yet.

## Not in this version

Level 2 and level select, unlocks, plane customization, breakup v2 and fuel-driven explosions, aerobatics, iOS and App Store release.
