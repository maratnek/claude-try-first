# Menu key art: the biplane and its pilot (2D image)

**Expected file:** `assets/ui/menu-keyart.png`
**Size:** 2048 x 1536 px, PNG with a transparent background (no sky, no ground).
**Used by:** the main menu, right half of the screen, next to the menu list.

## Prompt (paste into an image generator)

> Cartoon illustration in the style of a 1990s TV adventure cartoon: bold clean
> dark outlines, rounded friendly shapes, flat cel shading with one soft
> highlight, warm saturated colours. A 1920s biplane parked at a slight
> three-quarter angle, cream-coloured wings and fuselage with deep maroon trim
> and a maroon tail, wooden two-blade propeller, spoked wheels, visible struts
> and bracing wires. Standing in front of the lower wing, a cheerful young
> 1920s pilot: brown leather flight jacket, leather flying cap with goggles
> pushed up, long white silk scarf blowing to the side, riding breeches and
> tall boots, one hand resting on the wing, confident friendly smile. Full
> figure and the whole plane in frame, nothing cropped. Isolated on a fully
> transparent background, no text, no logo, no signature, no frame.

## Notes

- The plane should read as our in-game biplane (`assets/models/biplane-1920.glb`):
  cream wings and body, maroon trim, two wings with struts.
- Generic 1920s pilot, not any existing cartoon character.
- If the generator cannot do transparency, use a plain flat pure-green
  background (#00FF00) instead; it will be keyed out.
