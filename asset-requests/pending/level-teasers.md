# Level teaser cards for the campaign (2D images)

**Expected files** (640 x 400 px each, PNG, no transparency):
- `assets/ui/level-01-first-flight.png`
- `assets/ui/level-02-mountain-pass.png`
- `assets/ui/level-03-lake.png`

**Used by:** the level list in the menu. Locked levels are darkened and
desaturated by the game, so draw every card in full colour.

## Shared style (add to every prompt)

> Cartoon illustration in the style of a 1990s TV adventure cartoon, bold clean
> outlines, rounded shapes, flat cel shading, warm saturated colours, small
> cream-and-maroon 1920s biplane, no text, no logo.

## Prompts

1. **First flight:** the biplane taking off from a grass airfield past a
   wooden hangar and a windsock, big yellow hoop rings floating ahead in a
   sunny sky.
2. **Mountain pass:** the biplane weaving between tall rocky mountains with
   snowy peaks, a winding valley below, a few clouds below the peaks.
3. **The lake:** a 1920s seaplane with floats skimming low over a calm blue
   lake surrounded by pine forest, a wooden pier on the shore.
